# RichAPI

> GTM data API: 65+ endpoints, one key, REST + MCP. Explicit endpoint billing, credits never expire.

- REST: https://api.richapi.ai/api/v1/{endpoint}  (header: x-api-key)
- MCP:  https://mcp.richapi.ai/mcp  (35+ tools)
- Billing: fixed credits, prepaid, never expire. Endpoint rates are per call or per result.
- Tiers: Sandbox $0 (25 credits) · Starter $100 (5,000 credits) · Growth $450 (25,000 credits, 11% bonus) · Scale $800 (50,000 credits, 25% bonus) · Enterprise custom (committed volume)
- Credit value: 1 credit costs $0.020 on Starter, $0.018 on Growth and $0.016 on Scale, bonus credits included.
